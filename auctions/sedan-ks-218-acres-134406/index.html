About This Auction

Discover Your Ultimate Outdoor Retreat in Kansas There is no need to elbow your way into a hunting property that is overpressured and lacking the age and quality of deer you would like to hunt. Situated in one of Kansas' most pristine and private regions, this 4-mile stretch of land boasts no through roads, ensuring unmatched privacy and peace. The property is bordered by expansive neighboring parcels ranging from 2,000 to 4,200 acres, providing a sense of seclusion rarely found elsewhere. Adding to the seclusion, the larger ranch bordering the property on two sides is in a restrictive conservation easement. With an extensive creek system ranging for miles feeding a large watershed lake, and three additional ponds on-site, this property is a haven for avid hunters, anglers, and nature lovers alike. Kansas is renowned for its world-class deer hunting, and this property is no exception. The deer population here is thriving, with multiple mature bucks making this area their home. According to Boone and Crockett Club records, Kansas consistently ranks among the top states for producing trophy whitetails, with an impressive number of entries each year. The abundance of rubs, scrapes, and heavily used trails on this property is a testament to its draw for deer. Additionally, the terraced upland acreage offers an opportunity to create large food plots on long ago tilled ground. This unparalleled food source combined with native browse and acorns will attract wildlife from the expansive prairies year around.  For those who enjoy waterfowl hunting and fishing, the property delivers on every front. The watershed slough, creek and ponds provide ideal habitats for ducks, while the crappie fishing in the creek is sure to impress even the most seasoned angler. Imagine spending your mornings hunting ducks at sunrise and your afternoons casting a line in the creek, surrounded by the sounds and smells of nature. The large elevation changes, hardwood ridges, and creek bottom provide a variety of environments for both recreation and wildlife. The views from the southern ridge are nothing short of breathtaking, offering a panoramic overlook that spans for miles in every direction. This property also carries a rich history, evidenced by the old rock fences that stand as silent witnesses to generations past. While the land has seen some mineral activity in its time, production has long since ceased, and nature is reclaiming these areas. The result is a property that feels untouched and unspoiled, offering a true escape from the worries of modern life. Despite the remoteness, convenience is just around the corner, with Buck’s BBQ a short drive away to satisfy your hunger after a day in the field. If you’re considering building your dream retreat, the property includes a prepared build site on its eastern side. With electric lines already in place and water nearby, this site overlooks a picturesque 90-degree bend in the creek, providing an idyllic setting for a home or cabin. Whether you’re planning a weekend getaway, a hunting lodge, or a full-time residence, this location is ready to bring your vision to life. This property is more than just land; it’s an opportunity to reconnect with the outdoors and live life on your terms. The combination of excellent hunting, fishing, breathtaking scenery, and the potential for a return on investment makes it a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. Don’t miss the chance to make this extraordinary property your own. Highlights of the Property: Bordered by large properties (2,000–3,800 acres) for added seclusion. Over 4 miles without a through road, ensuring privacy. Creek feeding a watershed lake and three additional ponds for duck hunting and fishing. Thriving deer population with multiple mature bucks, perfect for trophy hunters. Terraced tillable acreage offering ROI potential and an exceptional food source for wildlife. Diverse terrain with hardwood ridges, creek bottoms, and breathtaking ridge-top views. Historic rock fences and untouched natural beauty. Prepped build site with electric and water access, overlooking the creek. Proximity to Buck’s BBQ for post-adventure meals.
