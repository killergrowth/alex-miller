With 225 +/-acres of fertile cropland, the farm is supported by desirable Class II and Class III soils, providing excellent long-term income potential. Approximately 32% of the farm is Class II soils and 61% is Class III soils, ensuring strong yields across the majority of the acreage. In addition, the property offers  77 +/- acres of recreational land that serves as prime habitat for deer, turkey, waterfowl and other wildlife.  The Verdigris River provides trophy fishing opportunities and enhances the farm’s natural appeal.  For buyers interested in conservation or habitat development, the soils on the property also presents the capability and potential to create wetlands and marshes.     An added benefit and valuable feature of this tract is its irrigation rights to pump directly from the Verdigris River for irrigation, allowing water to be pumped directly for crop production - further boosting the farm’s value and versatility.  Beyond farming, the river corridor provides abundant wildlife habitat, with thriving populations of deer, turkey, and waterfowl.  The Verdigris River itself offers excellent fishing and scenic beauty, making this a rare property that delivers both income potential and recreational value. Conveniently located just off Highway 75, the farm offers easy access to local grain elevators.  Both Independence and Neodesha, KS are within 10 minutes, providing quick access to local grain elevators for efficient marketing of crops.   Whether you are an investor, farmer, and/or outdoorsman, this property offers a unique opportunity to own a highly versatile and desirable tract of Kansas river-bottom land.     Property Highlights: Total Size:  302.32 acres along the Verdigris River Cropland:  225.6 acres of productive Class II & Class III soils. Potential and capability to create wetlands and marshes. Wildlife:  Abundant deer, turkey, and waterfowl populations.
